A local shop, not a dispatch center

Ironclad Locksmith started with one van and a phone that got forwarded to a kitchen table. Today it's a full team — but every technician still answers to the same standard the business was built on.

How we got here

Ironclad Locksmith opened in 2013 after its founder spent nine years as an apprentice and journeyman locksmith, watching too many neighbors get quoted one price on the phone and a much higher one on the doorstep. The business was built around a simple rule: the number you hear on the call is the number on the invoice.

That rule is still how the shop runs. As the team grew from one van to a full fleet, every new technician trains under a senior locksmith before taking a call alone, and every job — residential, automotive, or commercial — gets priced the same way: confirmed before the van leaves the lot.

What we hold every technician to

Licensed & bonded

Every locksmith carries current state licensing and is bonded before working a solo call.

Background-checked

A criminal background check is run before anyone is issued a company badge or van keys.

Priced before dispatch

You hear the price on the phone, in writing if you'd like it, before any technician leaves the shop.
